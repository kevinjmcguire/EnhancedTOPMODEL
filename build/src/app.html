TOPMODEL Watershed Explorer

Eleven CAMELS gauged watersheds with ten water years (Oct 2004 – Sep 2014) of Daymet forcing and USGS daily discharge. Four are in Virginia, one per physiographic province; the other seven span the main US climates. Topography for each comes from a 30 m 3DEP DEM. Pick one, then run, calibrate and test land-use change on the next pages.

About this version

      
A browser port of the Enhanced TOPMODEL R/Shiny app. The model core (Penman–Monteith PET, rain and snow interception, degree-day snow, optional stormflow zone, infiltration excess, impervious runoff, two-tile land-use change and channel routing) is translated line for line from topmodel_core.R; on test runs it reproduces the R output to within 1e-13 mm/d. SCE-UA calibration runs in a background thread.

      
Data

      
    
        
  • Forcing: Daymet basin means from CAMELS (Newman et al. 2015): precipitation, Tmax, Tmin, shortwave (srad × day length) and vapour pressure. No wind, so the wind parameter is used.
    • 
        
  • Discharge: USGS daily mean flow (CAMELS quality-controlled), converted to mm/d with the GAGES-II drainage area.
    • 
        
  • Attributes (area, slope, LAI, climate indices, land cover): CAMELS attributes v2.0 (Addor et al. 2017). Canopy starting values use the CAMELS MODIS LAI maximum and seasonal range.
    • 
      

      
Differences from the R app

      
    
        
  • Watersheds are fixed to these eleven examples; DEM delineation and the Daymet, NLCD and MODIS downloads are not available in the browser.
    • 
        
  • Topography is precomputed: 30 m 3DEP DEMs delineated at the gauges with twi_watershed_usgs.m (priority-flood, D8 watershed, MD-infinity TWI), 30 equal-width TWI classes as in the R app, and D8 channel distances to the outlet (200-bin histogram) for routing.
    • 
        
  • The saturated-area map is drawn at up to 320 pixels across (one map pixel = 1–8 DEM cells); a pixel's shade is the saturated share of its 30 m cells. Land-use change areas are placed by TWI class.
    • 
        
  • DREAMzs uncertainty analysis is not included; SCE-UA is.
    • 
        
  • The pre-calibrated sets come from SCE-UA (KGE, 3,000 runs, first 60% after warm-up) on m, ln Te, Srmax, td, DDF, rl and channel velocity, plus Ks with infiltration excess on for the two dryland basins.
    • 
        
  • Seco Creek and Cave Creek fit poorly even after calibration (validation KGE near or below 0). Ephemeral, storm-driven runoff from daily data with saturation-excess physics is a known weak spot of TOPMODEL; they are included to show it.
